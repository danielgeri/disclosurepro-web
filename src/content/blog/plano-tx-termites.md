## The Hidden Threat: Subterranean Termites in Plano and Collin County

Plano experiences extreme summer heat, mild winters, and intense seasonal downpours that drive dramatic moisture swings in regional soils.

### Why Plano's Soil and Slab-on-Grade Construction Foster Infestations

Several local geological and construction factors make Plano homes vulnerable to wood-destroying pests:
- **Expansive Black Gumbo Clay Soil:** Collin County rests on dense, expansive montmorillonite clay ("black gumbo"). During dry summer heatwaves, the clay shrinks and pulls away from concrete foundation grade beams. During heavy seasonal rainstorms, the clay expands rapidly, exerting hydraulic pressure that produces hairline settling cracks across residential concrete slabs.
- **Slab Penetrations and Expansion Joints:** Monolithic and post-tension concrete slabs feature numerous utility penetrations for sewer pipes, bathtub drain traps, and plumbing manifolds. **Eastern Subterranean Termites (*Reticulitermes flavipes*)** and increasingly prevalent **Formosan Subterranean Termites (*Coptotermes formosanus*)** exploit hairline concrete fractures as narrow as 1/32 of an inch to enter bottom wall plates.
- **Brick Veneer Weep Holes:** Building codes require vertical open weep holes along the bottom course of brick veneer to ventilate wall cavities. When homeowners or landscape contractors add mulch, flower beds, or decorative stone above the brick ledge, termites travel directly through unshielded weep holes into exterior stud framing.
- **Lawn Irrigation Microclimates:** Automated sprinkler systems running daily along foundation perimeters keep the surrounding clay damp, providing continuous hydration for massive underground termite colonies.

When irrigated foundation soils meet slab settling cracks, subterranean termites establish massive colonies beneath luxury residences.

### The Biology and Mechanics of Subterranean Termites in North Texas

Subterranean termites live in structured underground colonies:
1. **Soil Hydration and Heat Avoidance:** Subterranean termites must maintain constant contact with soil moisture to prevent dehydration. In summer, colonies forage deep beneath concrete slabs where moisture from plumbing lines accumulates.
2. **Mud Shelter Tubes:** To bridge exposed foundation surfaces, worker termites construct airtight mud tubes made of digested wood, clay particles, and saliva.
3. **Internal Structural Consumption:** Termites hollow out bottom sole plates, load-bearing 2x4 and 2x6 wall studs, and headers from the inside out along the softer springwood grain. Structural wall framing can appear completely sound behind drywall while being completely hollowed out internally.

Because structural decay occurs within sealed wall cavities behind drywall, severe load-bearing damage often remains hidden until baseboards soften or doorframes bind.

## Texas Real Estate Disclosure Laws: What Plano Buyers Must Know

Residential real estate transfers in Texas are governed by **Texas Property Code § 5.008**, which requires sellers of single-family residential property to deliver a written Seller's Disclosure Notice to prospective buyers before executing a purchase contract.

### Key Texas Disclosure Documents

When buying a home in Plano, buyers should review several standard statutory and contractual documents:
- **Texas REALTORS® Seller's Disclosure Notice (Form TXR 1406) / TREC Form OP-H:** Mandated under Texas Property Code § 5.008. Section 3 and Section 4 explicitly address *"Termites / Wood Destroying Insects"*, requiring sellers to disclose:
  - Any active infestation of termites or other wood-destroying insects.
  - Any previous termite treatment or preventative chemical application.
  - Any previous damage from termites or wood-destroying insects.
  - Any previous termite repairs completed to structural framing.
  - Whether there is an active, transferable termite warranty or service contract on the property.
- **Texas Official Wood Destroying Insect (WDI) Report:** Mandated by the Texas Department of Agriculture Structural Pest Control Service (SPCS). Required by FHA, VA, and most conventional lenders in Collin County. A licensed Texas pest inspector conducts a visual assessment of accessible framing, brick weep holes, and foundation perimeters.
- **TREC One to Four Family Residential Contract (Resale):** Paragraph 7(B) governs the delivery and review of the Seller's Disclosure Notice, granting buyers the statutory right to terminate the contract and receive a full refund of earnest money if the notice is not delivered within the agreed timeline.

### What Texas Sellers Reveal vs. Conceal

Under Texas Property Code § 5.008, sellers must complete the disclosure notice based on their actual knowledge. However, sellers often use ambiguous phrasing to downplay past infestations:
- *"Completed preventative perimeter barrier treatment in 2023; annual warranty transferable to buyer"* (omitting that the 2023 treatment followed an active subterranean breakout inside the living room wall).
- *"Replaced baseboards and patched drywall during interior remodeling"* (masking that the studs behind the drywall were hollowed out by subterranean termites).
- *"Seller is an investor/relocation company with limited occupancy history"* (while certain corporate sellers may claim limited knowledge, intentional concealment of known latent material defects remains actionable fraud under the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act).

## The Manual Review Problem: Red Flags in Massive Documentation

In a typical Plano home purchase, the complete due diligence packet often spans 150 to 250 pages. This package includes:
- The completed Texas REALTORS® Seller's Disclosure Notice
- Multi-page general home inspection reports containing 80+ high-resolution photos
- Texas SPCS Official WDI inspection reports and treatment warranty documents
- Plano HOA CC&Rs, bylaws, and financial reserve statements
- Collin County Appraisal District records, property surveys, and title commitments

### How Critical Warnings Get Overlooked

Crucial indications of termite damage are frequently buried in technical inspection comments:
- An inspector might write: *"Brick veneer weep holes covered by landscape mulch along south and east elevations; slab edge inaccessible for visual probing."* If a buyer overlooks this caveat, they assume all structural framing is healthy.
- An attached invoice might itemize *"slab core drilling and foam termiticide injection beneath master bath plumbing,"* confirming a severe prior infestation that the seller described as routine maintenance.

Manually cross-referencing hundreds of dense pages during a compressed contractual Option Period creates substantial risk for homebuyers.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Termites in Plano

**1. Are termites common in Plano and Collin County, Texas?**
Yes. Eastern Subterranean Termites and Formosan Subterranean Termites are highly active throughout Plano and North Texas. The combination of expansive black gumbo clay soil, slab settling cracks, and brick weep holes provides continuous access to residential wood framing.

**2. Does Texas law require a termite inspection before buying a house?**
No. Texas state law does not mandate a dedicated pest inspection for conventional cash or private mortgage sales. However, FHA and VA loan programs require an Official Texas Wood Destroying Insect (WDI) Report completed by a licensed Texas Structural Pest Control Service technician.

**3. What does Texas Property Code § 5.008 require a seller to disclose regarding termites?**
Under Texas Property Code § 5.008, sellers must disclose all known active infestations, previous chemical treatments, previous termite damage, and whether an active termite service contract or warranty exists on the home.

**4. What is the Texas "Option Period" and how does it protect buyers?**
Under Paragraph 5 of the TREC One to Four Family Residential Contract, buyers purchase a negotiated "Option Period" (typically 5 to 10 days) granting the unrestricted right to terminate the contract for any reason. This window is the buyer's critical opportunity to conduct specialized WDI inspections and review all disclosure documents.
